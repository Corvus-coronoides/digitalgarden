$d\star J^{(p+1)}=0$  charge: $Q^{(p)}=\int_{\Sigma_{d-p-1}}\star J^{(p+1)}$
   eg. Maxwell in 4d
      $\star J_{e}^{(2)}=\frac{i}{e^2}\star F^{(2)}=\frac{i}{e^2} D A_{D}, \star J_{m}^{(2)}=\frac{1}{2\pi} F^{(2)}$
      $A\rightarrow A+\lambda$ with $d\lambda=0$, where $\lambda$ is a globally defined closed 1-form, while gauge transformation is $A\rightarrow A+d\lambda'$ (exact 1-form)
