1. gauge a finte group/discrete symmetry
    ~discrete Fourier transformation of the partition function $Z(\mathscr{T},J)$: theory $\mathscr{T}$ with global [[p-form symmetry\|p-form symmetry]] $G^{(p)}$ and associated BG currents $J_{p+1}$,
    $Z(\mathscr{T}/G^{(p)},\hat{J}_{d-p-1})\sim\sum_{J_{p+1}\in H^{p+1}(M_d,G^{(p)})}e^{2\pi i(\hat{J},J)}Z(\mathscr{T},J_{p+1})$
    where $e^{2\pi i(\hat{B},B)}$ is coupling the dual theory $\mathscr{T}/G^{(p)}$ to the current of $J$ (or a pairing between $J\in H^{p+1}(M_d,G^{(p)})$ and $\hat{J}=\star J\in H^{d-p-1}(M_d,\hat{G}^{(d-p-2)})\simeq H_{p+1}$, [[Poincare duality\|Poincare duality]]) and is the weight in the Fourier transform.
    The dual symmetry $\hat{G}^{(d-p-2)}$ is a $d-p-2$-form symmetry, the [[Pontryagin dual\|Pontryagin dual]] of $G$: $\hat{G}=Hom(G,U(1))$. It provides a explicit expression for the $p$-form symmetry generator: (just charges for the $p$-form one)
    $D_{d-p-1}=exp(i\int_{M_{d-p-1}}\hat{J}_{d-p-1})$
    which acts on/links with $p$-dim operators. Its own generator is given by $(M_{p+1},J_{p+1})$.
