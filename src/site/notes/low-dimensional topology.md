1-manifold: homeomorphic to a line or a circle;
2-manifold: Classification Theorem of (Closed) Surfaces--closed surfaces must be either spheres, connected sum of tori, or connected sum of projective planes. The former two are orientable and are classified by [[genus\|genus]], while the latter is unorientable.
3-manifold: Moise Theorem--every topological 3-manifold has a unique piecewise-linear structure and [[smooth structure\|smooth structure]]. Thurston's geometrization theorem. 
    Physics: [[Chern-Simons\|Chern-Simons]]-[[Jones polynomials\|Jones polynomials]]; Gukov-Pei-Putrov-Vafa ([[GPPV\|GPPV]]) invariant-6d (2,0) theory and its compactifications.
4-manifold: open problem when involving smooth structures. 
     eg. [[Donaldson invariant\|Donaldson invariant]], [[Seiberg-Witten\|Seiberg-Witten]] invariant, [[intersection theory\|intersection theory]].
(others and $\geq$ 5d: h-cobordism theorem and [[surgery\|surgery]] theory)
