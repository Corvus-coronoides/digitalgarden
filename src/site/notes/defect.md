universal fields (focus): considering worldline of defect $M\rightarrow X\simeq \mathbb{R}^{D}$, they should be $\sigma$-field (pullback of metric on $X$; still bulk field) and structure of normal bundle.
Suppose metric is fixed, topological,
$M\simeq \mathbb{R}^{d}\cup \{\infty\}\simeq S^d$, while the change of framing (normal vector field) is given by $M\rightarrow SO(D-d)$, such that $\pi_{d}(SO(D-d))^{\vee}$ ([[Pontryagin dual\|Pontryagin dual]]) classifies the change of framing.
